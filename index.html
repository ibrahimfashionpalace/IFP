Terms & Conditions

1. Cash on Delivery (COD) All orders placed through IBRAHIM FASHION PALACE are fulfilled via Cash on Delivery. Full payment in cash must be made to our delivery partner upon receipt of your parcel.
2. OTP Verification at Doorstep A secure 4-digit OTP is assigned to each order. Share this OTP with the delivery partner only when you are physically receiving the parcel. Handing over the OTP serves as formal proof of delivery.
3. Order Cancellation Policy Customers can cancel their orders directly from the "My Orders" tab within 6 hours of placing the order. Once dispatched or after 6 hours, cancellations cannot be processed through the app.
4. Size Exchange & Returns Exchanges for size fit issues are supported within 24 hours of delivery, provided garments are unworn, undamaged, and retained in original packaging and the parcel is to be returned by the customer by himself at our offline store.
5. Customer Support For any questions or order inquiries, reach us anytime via Call/WhatsApp at 6005757393 or email aamir757393@gmail.com.
